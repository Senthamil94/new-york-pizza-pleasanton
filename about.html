Our story

Big city flavor, around the corner

At New York Pizza, we’re passionate about bringing the authentic flavors of classic New York-style pizza to California. From our crispy thin crust to our rich tomato sauce and hand-picked fresh toppings, we bring you the taste that made New York pizza famous worldwide.

Every pizza is crafted with love and premium ingredients. Whether you’re grabbing a quick slice or ordering for the whole family, we pride ourselves on fast, friendly service and a meal you’ll remember.

Join us for a taste of New York that’s just around the corner.
