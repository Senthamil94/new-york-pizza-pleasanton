How it works

Three steps to a full table

  1. 1. Pick a package

    Choose by guest count. Tell us your pizza picks and wing sauces — mix and match freely.

  2. 2. Order catering

    Order online (opens in a new tab) with your date, time and delivery address or pickup time. 24 hours ahead is best.

  3. 3. We bring the heat

    Pizzas come sliced and boxed, wings and knots in trays, salads with dressing on the side. Plates and napkins included on request.

Good to know

The fine print

  • Serving sizes: large pizzas are 8 slices; “feeds” assumes 2–3 slices plus sides per guest.
  • Halal: the Halal Celebration is fully halal; halal pizzas can be swapped into any package for a small upcharge.
  • Dietary: vegetarian swaps are free; gluten-free crust available on request.
  • Delivery: Pleasanton and nearby. Delivery fee and tax calculated at booking.
  • Notice: 24 hours preferred; same-day orders welcome when the ovens allow.
