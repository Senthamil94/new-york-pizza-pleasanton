Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where is New York Pizza located?

We’re at 690 Main Street, Pleasanton, CA 94566, in downtown Pleasanton. Get directions (opens in a new tab).

What are your hours?

Sunday to Thursday from 10 AM to 10:30 PM, and Friday and Saturday from 10 AM to 12 AM.

Can I order online for pickup or delivery?

Yes. Order online (opens in a new tab) for quick pickup or delivery at restaurant prices.

Do you have a halal menu?

Yes. Our halal menu includes Desi Pizza, Garlic Chicken and Pesto Chicken halal pizzas, Halal Beef Pepperoni Poppers and a halal Meatball Sub.

Do you sell pizza by the slice?

Yes. A classic cheese slice is $7.99 and a specialty slice is $12.99. See By the Slice.

Do you offer a gluten-free crust?

Yes, a gluten-free crust is available. If you have an allergy, please call us at (925) 484-4757 before ordering.

Do you offer catering?

Catering is coming soon. For group orders today, our Large and Extra Large pizzas each feed 4–5 people.
